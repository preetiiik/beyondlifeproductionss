The Route

From hello to
happily ever after.

Every couple travels the same five stations with us. The pace is yours; we just make sure nothing on the way is missed.

  1. Months beforeStation 01

    Hello

    A call or a chai. We listen to how you met, who’s coming, and which rituals matter most.

  2. Weeks beforeStation 02

    The Plan

    Timelines, venues and shot lists, down to the cousin who must be in the baraat photo.

  3. Before the dayStation 03

    Just Us Two

    A pre-wedding shoot, so the camera feels like an old friend when the big day comes.

  4. The dayStation 04

    The Wedding

    Haldi to vidaai. We stay quiet, close and out of the way, and catch what’s real.

  5. AfterStation 05

    Return Ticket

    Your film, the edited photographs and an heirloom album to go back to, again and again.
